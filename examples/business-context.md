# Capital markets meeting summary use case

The readers are institutional sales professionals preparing for advisor and client
conversations after a weekly capital markets meeting summarized by Zoom AI.

The summary should help readers quickly understand material economic and market
developments, significant figures and time horizons, investment perspectives, risks and
qualifications, relevant implications for advisor conversations, and consequential
questions and answers. Brief takeaways and supporting detail serve different reading
needs; useful expansion is not inherently redundant.

Readers may use the summary to plan exploratory outreach. Distinguish suggestions derived
from meeting insights from actions or recommendations actually discussed or agreed.
Attribution matters when it changes whether a statement represents a participant, a
presenter, or an institution. Do not turn a tentative outlook into an endorsed investment
recommendation.

These goals describe reader needs. They do not require headings, section order, a fixed
number of bullets, or a particular summary format, and they supply no factual evidence.
